Details, pros and cons

Canada Goose Expedition Parka

Best for: extreme cold, long outdoor waits, exposed work. Not best for: errands, transit, tight budgets.

The original extreme-weather parka, developed for scientists working in Antarctica. TEI5 rated. In published warmth testing it earned the highest warmth score of the parkas compared.

Pros: warmest in its class; long coverage; roomy protective cut; made in Canada.
Cons: near $2,000 retail; bulky for everyday city use; overkill if your winter is mostly short trips.

Check it at The Outfitters → — women's Expedition listed at $1,595 CAD closeout, price checked November 4, 2026.

Arc'teryx Therme Parka

Best for: wet, windy Canadian cities. Not best for: tight budgets, or standing still for hours at −30°C.

Hybrid insulation — 750-fill goose down in the core and sleeves, synthetic Coreloft at the underarms, cuffs and hood where sweat and snowmelt would collapse pure down — under a 2-layer GORE-TEX shell.

Pros: weather protection plus insulation; cleaner urban silhouette; hybrid fill handles damp spots.
Cons: price varies widely by retailer and model variant; not the warmest choice for extreme standing cold.

The North Face McMurdo Parka

Best for: cold-city everyday winter. Not best for: long inactive stretches at −30°C without a warm base layer.

Long insulated shape, 600-fill recycled down, DryVent 2L shell, protective hood and an everyday fit — useful coverage for the price.

Pros: mid price; long coverage; protective hood; easy everyday fit.
Cons: add a warm base layer for long, inactive time at −30°C; not expedition-grade.

Check it at Ernie's Sports Experts → men's McMurdo listed at $539.99, price checked November 4, 2026.

Columbia Marquam Peak Fusion II Parka

Best for: everyday winter on a budget. Not best for: extreme exposure or deep cold as a standalone.

Synthetic-insulated everyday winter parka with a hooded, longer silhouette — warmth without a luxury price tag.

Pros: lowest price on this page; hooded longer cut; easy care synthetic fill.
Cons: synthetic fill is less warm per weight than down; not built for extreme exposure.

Check it at Ernie's Sports Experts → men's Marquam Peak Fusion II listed at $299.99, price checked November 4, 2026.

How these listings work

We do not copy retailer photos or descriptions, and we do not guess links — every link above is a real retailer page. More retailers are added per product only as their links are verified. If any retailer asks for a listing to be removed, it is removed. Affiliate links, when added, will be clearly marked; none are active yet.
