Which parka is right for your winter?

For −30°C, a long expedition-style parka with a sealing hood, room for a midlayer and strong wind protection matters more than any single spec — coverage, hood design and how long you stand outside count as much as the insulation inside. For city winters with shorter exposure, a mid-weight parka plus a warm base layer is usually enough.

Temperature ratings, honestly

Canada Goose publishes its own Thermal Experience Index: TEI5 is −22°F and below (about −30°C), TEI4 is 5°F to −13°F (about −15°C to −25°C), and TEI3 is 14°F to −4°F (about −10°C to −20°C). Other brands publish their own ratings the same way — for example, some Canadian independents rate parkas to −30°C or −35°C. These are brand claims, not one shared standard, so treat a rating as a guide, not a guarantee. People who feel the cold easily should choose a warmer rating than the chart suggests.

Canadian brands: made vs designed in Canada

Kanuk (Montreal, 1974) manufactures in Montreal and Quartz Co. (Montreal, 1997) manufactures in Quebec — genuinely made in Canada. Mackage (Montreal, 1999) is designed in Montreal but made by partner factories abroad. Canada Goose (Toronto, 1957) brands its products Made in Canada with factories in Toronto, Winnipeg and Montreal. Under Competition Bureau guidance, "Made in Canada" means at least 51% of total direct costs were incurred here — check the label for a qualifying statement such as "Made in Canada with imported parts." Other Canadian-origin names: Arc'teryx, Moose Knuckles, Nobis, SOIA & KYO, Aritzia, Rudsak, Indyeva, Ookpik, Chlorophylle, Orage and Lolë.

Frequently asked questions

What is the warmest parka for −30°C in Canada?

For −30°C, look for a long expedition-style parka with a sealing hood, room for a midlayer, and strong wind protection, such as the Canada Goose Expedition Parka or Arc'teryx Therme SV. Coverage, hood design and how long you stand outside matter as much as the insulation inside.

What is a good winter jacket under $600 in Canada?

Under $600, the North Face McMurdo Parka at about $500 and the Columbia Marquam Peak Fusion Parka at about $450 are popular cold-city styles, and the Mountain Warehouse Extreme Expedition Parka at about $300 is a budget pick for occasional deep cold. Add a warm base layer for long inactive stretches at −30°C.

Which Canadian jacket brands are actually made in Canada?

Kanuk manufactures in Montreal and Quartz Co. manufactures in Quebec, while brands like Mackage are designed in Montreal but made by partner factories abroad. Under Competition Bureau guidance, Made in Canada means at least 51% of total direct costs were incurred in Canada.
