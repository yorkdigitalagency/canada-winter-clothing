What we do

  1. Start from published specifications — insulation type, fill power, shell material, cut and the brand's own temperature guidance.
  2. Read professional reviews and user reviews, good and bad — what holds up over time matters more than launch praise.
  3. Compare by job, not by one winner — extreme cold, everyday value and wet winters are different jobs, so each gets its own pick.
  4. Check real retailer listings — every outbound link is a verified retailer page, and every price carries the date it was checked.
  5. State the limits — brand temperature ratings are brand claims, not a shared standard, and we say so wherever a rating appears.

What we do not do

  • No hands-on testing claims. We do not test jackets ourselves and we publish no test scores. Any score you see quoted here is attributed to the tester who produced it.
  • No fake reviewers. This site has no named reviewer persona. Recommendations come from documented research, not from a person who does not exist.
  • No copied content. No retailer photos, no retailer descriptions, no scraped listings. Summaries are written here, in our own words.
  • No guessed links or prices. A retailer is listed only with a verified link and a checked price. Where only one verified retailer exists, the page says so.
  • No fake certainty. Where sources disagree — and on warmth, they often do — the page says they disagree.

How picks are labelled

Every pick states best for and not best for, plus pros and cons. A pick labelled "best value" means the best balance of warmth and price in published research, not the cheapest item and not a tested winner. If a better-documented option appears, the pick changes and the page date changes with it.

Prices and money

Listing prices are checked on the date shown beside them and shift with size, colour and sales. Listings are re-checked when pages are updated. This site sells nothing: purchases happen on the retailer's own page. Affiliate links, when added, will be clearly marked; none are active yet. Display-ad slots on this site are placeholders until an ad network approves the site.

Corrections and removals

If a price, specification or link is wrong, or if any retailer or brand asks for a listing to be removed, it is corrected or removed. Pages carry an updated date so you can see how fresh the information is.

Frequently asked questions

Do you test the jackets yourselves?

No. Winter Clothing Canada does not do hands-on testing, and it does not publish test scores. Picks come from documented research: published specifications, professional reviews, user reviews both good and bad, and retailer listings, with the sources stated on each page.

How do you choose a pick?

Picks are chosen by job, not by one overall winner: best for extreme cold, best value, and best for wet winters. Each pick states who it is best for and who it is not best for, with pros and cons drawn from published research.

How current are your prices?

Every listing price carries the date it was checked. Prices shift with size, colour and sales, so the retailer's own page is always the final word. Listings are re-checked when pages are updated, and any retailer can ask for a listing to be removed.
